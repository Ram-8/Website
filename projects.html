Misere Tic-Tac-Toe

May 2024

An Android implementation of Misere Tic-Tac-Toe, a variant of the classic game where the objective is inverted: the player who makes three in a row loses the game. In this game two players can play against each other either in the same device or on different devices. Built with Jetpack Compose and Material Design 3.
